# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Loads on any NeoForge (or Forge) build of its Minecraft version, not only the newest: 1.0.0 asked for the
  newest and would not load in packs on older ones (All the Mods 10's NeoForge 21.1.250, for one).

## 1.0.0

- First release: mute any particle, Minecraft's or a mod's, from a button in the survival and creative inventories
  (beside Extreme Sound Muffler's when it is installed).
- Lists: Recent (busiest first), All (by mod), Muted, Mods; search; pictures and live counts for every particle; a
  master switch; Unmute all.
- Block breaking, block mining and item pickup particles can be muted too.
- NeoForge 26.3, 26.1.2 and 1.21.1; Forge 1.20.1.
